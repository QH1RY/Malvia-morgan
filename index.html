Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Answers to common questions about working with Malvia and Quirky Hire.

What does Malvia specialise in?

Luxury venue draping, creative event styling, bespoke installations and immersive brand experiences for corporate events, weddings and private celebrations.

Do you offer luxury venue draping in London?

Yes. We create professional wall draping, ceiling draping, fabric backdrops and full venue transformations for London event spaces, hotels and private venues.

Can you design and install a complete event setup?

Yes. Depending on your project, Quirky Hire can manage concept development, sourcing, styling, installation, staffing and dismantling.

Do you work outside London?

Yes. We are London-based and take on suitable projects across the UK.

Can you combine styling with photo booths or interactive games?

Yes. We can bring together event styling, custom photo opportunities, AI photo booths and interactive branded games as one coordinated event experience.
