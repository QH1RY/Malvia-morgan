Frequently asked questions

Good to know before ordering.

Are these made-to-order drapes or a draping hire service?

All panels are made to order for purchase, not hire. For fully managed event styling and installation, visit Quirky Hire.

How long does production take?

Our typical made-to-order production time is 3–5 working days after the specification is confirmed. Delivery time is additional, and estimated dispatch dates are confirmed with your quotation.

Can I request specific sizes and colours?

Yes. Include your preferred finished height, panel width, quantity and colour in your enquiry. Availability and any made-to-order requirements will be confirmed.

Are the drapes fire retardant?

Fire-retardancy status and supporting certificates depend on the specific fabric and batch. Request the documentation you need before ordering; no certification is implied unless explicitly confirmed.

Do you deliver across the UK?

UK delivery can be discussed when you enquire. Shipping cost, destination coverage and lead times will be confirmed with your quote.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes, please include the number of panels and any repeat-order requirements so we can review your enquiry.
